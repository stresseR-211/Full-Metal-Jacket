# --- Full Metal Jacket ---
### Full Metal Jacket is a Bukkit plugin that will make the players hate your server. It adds:
- the fatigue
- weight to the objects
- dehydration
- hypothermia & hyperthermia
- increased damage
- injuries
- backpacks and pockets (in exchange of your inventory)
- custom items:
	- salt & salted food
	- knives
	- blood
	- alcohol
	- currency (banknotes + coins)
	- new material: steel, ruby, emerald
- hunger rework
- potion system rework
- redstone rework
___
### Dependencies: a Bukkit-compatible server, FMJ texture pack
### Supported (tested) servers: Paper
### Supported versions: 1.20.1
